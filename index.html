Class Strengths / 全班強項 (Evidence-Based)

  • Water Temperature & Speed: All groups successfully observed that hot water dissolves salt much faster than cold water. (全班各組均能透過實測發現熱水溶解食鹽的速度遠快於冷水。)
  • Controlled Variables: Strong awareness that water volume (100 mL) and salt mass (5.0 g) must be strictly kept constant. (具備良好的控制變因意識，理解水量與食鹽質量必須嚴格保持恆定。)

Growth Areas & Teacher Focus / 需要改進之處與教學著力點

  • Human Timing vs Systematic Error: Differentiating physiological stopwatch reaction delays from apparatus stirring inconsistencies. (需加強區分按碼錶的生理反應時差與手動攪拌不均等儀器/人為操作誤差。)
  • Class Average Value: Explaining why taking the average of 8 groups cancels out individual timing errors to increase reliability. (需引導學生深刻用自己的語言解釋計算全班 8 組平均值如何抵消個人誤差、提高科學信度。)
